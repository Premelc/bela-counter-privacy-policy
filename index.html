Belot Counter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Belot Counter (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it. The app is an offline score counter for the card game Belot. It is built to work without accounts and without collecting personal information about you.

Information we do not collect

The app does not require you to sign in or create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information. We do not collect personal identification information about you.

Game data stays on your device

Your scores, team names, round and game history, and preferences (such as language and target points) are stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us or synced to any server, and it is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process non-personal information such as your device type, advertising identifier, approximate (coarse) location, and ad-interaction data.

  • On first launch — and wherever required by law, such as in the EEA and the UK — you are shown a consent form where you can accept or manage personalized advertising.
  • On iOS you are also asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking identifier is used.
  • If you decline personalized ads, the app still shows non-personalized ads.
  • You can change your advertising choices at any time from the app’s About screen (“Ad privacy options”).

Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Camera and photos

If you choose to capture or attach a photo when sharing a game result, the app requests access to your camera and/or photo library. Photos are used only to create the image you decide to share and are not sent to us. You can decline these permissions in your device settings; the rest of the app will continue to work.

Bug reports

If you use the in-app “Report a bug” feature, the message you write — together with any details or image you choose to attach and basic technical context about the app — is sent to our support service so we can investigate the issue. Please avoid including personal information in a bug report.

How information is shared

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal identification information to anyone. Non-personal information may be processed by the third-party services described above (Google AdMob) solely to provide their functionality.

Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures, aligned with the GDPR, to protect the limited information handled through the app.

International data transfers

Where information originating in the EEA is processed in another country, appropriate safeguards — such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses — are relied upon by the third-party services involved.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights and choices

You can reset your ad consent from the app’s About screen, adjust tracking and permission settings at the operating-system level, and remove all locally stored data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have rights to access, rectify, and erase information relating to you — contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the current version, and continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
